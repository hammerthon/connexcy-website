FAQ

Questions, answered

What kinds of contracts can Connexcy manage?

Any recurring vendor agreement: SaaS subscriptions, telecom and internet, cloud hosting, facilities and leases, equipment, maintenance, insurance, staffing and professional services.

How do my contracts get into Connexcy?

Upload PDFs, forward emails, or share a folder. During the beta our team helps with onboarding and checks that every key date and term is captured correctly.

What does "we distribute renewals for you" mean?

When a contract is coming up, Connexcy sends it with a summary and deadline to the people who need to decide (and to the vendor when appropriate), then follows up until there's a decision.

How does overage detection work?

Each invoice is compared with the rates and quantities in its contract. Anything that doesn't match, like a price increase, duplicate line or out-of-contract fee, is flagged for review.

Is my data secure?

Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited by role, and we never share your contract data with third parties.
