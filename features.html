  • Auto-renewal clauses and notice periods highlighted
  • Email and calendar reminders for each contract owner
  • A renewals timeline for the next 12 months

Automated distribution

Reminders don't help if they sit unread. Connexcy sends each renewal to the people who need to decide, with a plain-English summary, and follows up until it's done.

  • Send contracts and renewals to stakeholders and vendors
  • Summary of terms, cost and deadline with every request
  • Automatic follow-ups and escalation
  • Track who has reviewed, approved or signed

Overage and billing-error detection

Small billing mistakes add up quietly. Connexcy flags anything that doesn't match the contract so you can dispute it before you pay.

  • Price increases above contracted rates
  • Duplicate charges and out-of-contract fees
  • Unused licenses and seats you're still paying for
  • Renewals you didn't approve

Approval hierarchy

Quotes and renewals go through your approval chain in order, and everyone can see where each one stands.

  • Approval rules by amount and category (e.g. IT Manager → Controller → CEO over $40k)
  • Profile photos show who approved, who's reviewing now and who's up next
  • Approval history on every contract, including who approved last
  • One-click nudges by email and in-app notification
  • A heads-up on who will need to approve upcoming renewals
