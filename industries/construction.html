What slips through

Where contractors lose money on vendors

Most companies don't lose money on bad deals. They lose it on deals nobody was watching.

Insurance certificates that lapse

Subcontractors' certificates of insurance expire mid-project, and nobody notices until there's a claim.

Rentals that run long

Equipment stays on site after the job and keeps billing, often at day rates instead of the weekly or monthly rate you agreed.

Agreements that roll over

Dumpsters, fleet tracking and phones renew for years at a time unless someone gives notice.

Every job, separately

Each job has its own rentals, dumpsters and suppliers, with invoices that arrive weeks later.
