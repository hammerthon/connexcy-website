What slips through

Where software companies lose money on vendors

Most companies don't lose money on bad deals. They lose it on deals nobody was watching.

Seats nobody uses

Licenses are bought for a hiring plan and renewed at the same count, long after people move on.

Usage past the commitment

Cloud, monitoring and data tools bill on-demand rates once usage runs past what you committed to.

Notice periods in the fine print

A 60-day notice period on a March renewal means the decision is due on New Year's Eve.

Owners spread across teams

Engineering, RevOps and IT each buy their own tools, so nobody sees every renewal at once.
