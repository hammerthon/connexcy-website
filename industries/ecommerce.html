What slips through

Where e-commerce brands lose money on vendors

Most companies don't lose money on bad deals. They lose it on deals nobody was watching.

Bills that grow on their own

Email, SMS and subscription apps price by contacts and orders, so the bill jumps a tier without anyone deciding.

Renewals in peak season

A warehouse or shipping agreement that renews in the fall leaves no time to switch before the holidays.

App sprawl

Every new app is a new contract, and the ones you stopped using keep billing.

Fees that don't match the agreement

Pick, pack, storage and shipping fees drift from the rates you negotiated.
