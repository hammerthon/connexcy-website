What slips through

Where financial services firms lose money on vendors

Most companies don't lose money on bad deals. They lose it on deals nobody was watching.

Multi-year auto-renewals

Market data and research agreements often renew for one or two years at a time, with 60 to 90 days' notice.

Increases above the cap

Renewal quotes can come in above the yearly increase your contract allows.

Why did we choose this vendor?

When an examiner or auditor asks, the answer is in someone's inbox, or gone with someone who left.

Contracts across the firm

Investments, operations and compliance each own vendors, with no single view of what renews when.
